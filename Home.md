## Start Here (Newcomers)

This wiki is organized around **Superagency** — the idea that AI can give more people access to expertise, leverage, creativity, and decision support that used to be scarce. It is not about replacement. It is about amplification.

If you're new, we recommend this path:

1. **[[Superagency]]** — the central organizing idea (3 min)
2. **[[Magnifica Humanitas]]** — the 2026 papal encyclical: "disarming technology" as a Superagency frame (5 min)
3. **[[Human Agency]]** — why this wiki focuses on human capability, not technology for its own sake (3 min)
4. **[[AI Agent Revolution]]** — Claude Code, OpenClaw, and the agent paradigm shift (4 min)
5. **[[Co-Intelligence]]** — everyday human-AI collaboration applied to creative, professional, and organizational work (4 min)
6. **[[Responsible Deployment]]** — the practical governance loop: use, measure, improve, govern (4 min)
7. Then explore [[Education]], [[Work]], or [[Creativity]] depending on your domain.

Prefer a map instead? Jump to [[#section-summaries|Section Summaries]] below.
