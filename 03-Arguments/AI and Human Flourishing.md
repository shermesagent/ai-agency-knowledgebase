# AI and Human Flourishing

## Core Idea
AI supports human flourishing when it expands time, creativity, learning, connection, health, meaningful work, and self-direction — and when it is designed to reward and develop human expertise rather than replace it.

## Why It Matters
The core question of the Superagency wiki is whether AI expands or contracts human agency. Flourishing is the ultimate measure: does AI help people live lives they have reason to value? Recent research provides both encouraging signals and important caveats. AI can democratize access to professional resources (the scientific collaboration RCT), but it can also amplify existing expertise gaps on complex tasks. Students overestimate their AI literacy while lacking operational skills. Humanlike AI design can feel empowering or surveilling depending on implementation. The flourishing case for AI depends on intentional design choices, not automatic benefits.

## Best Supporting Sources
- [Positive Alignment: Artificial Intelligence for Human Flourishing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10310), Laukkonen et al., 2026 — proposes that AI alignment should include positive support for pluralistic human and ecological flourishing, not only safeguards against harm.
- [Human-AI Collaboration in Science at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24180), Wang et al., 2026 — causal evidence that AI feedback increases scientific revision rates by 12.55%, with strongest benefits for researchers with least access to traditional feedback.
- [AI as Equalizer or Amplifier?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10961), An, 2025/2026 — reconciles equalizer and amplifier perspectives: AI narrows gaps on routine tasks, widens them on complex tasks.
- [The Illusion of Competence: European Students' AI Readiness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26010), Rodriguez-Alvarez et al., 2026 — documents Dunning-Kruger effect in student AI literacy; 76.5% demand pedagogical reform toward hands-on creation.
- [After Orthogonality: Virtue-Ethical Agency and AI Alignment](https://thegradient.pub/virtue-ethics-ai-alignment/), The Gradient, 2026 — virtue-ethical framework for AI alignment centering on practical wisdom and agency.
- [Superagency](https://www.superagency.ai/), Reid Hoffman with Greg Beato, 2025 — foundational optimistic thesis for AI as agency-expanding technology.

## Practical Examples
- Design AI tools that surface uncertainty and alternatives rather than presenting single authoritative answers — this builds calibrated trust rather than false confidence.
- When deploying AI in educational or professional contexts, pair tool access with domain expertise development — don't just give people AI, invest in their knowledge.
- Audit personal AI use for the "surveillance effect": if an AI tool makes you feel watched rather than supported, redesign the interaction pattern.
- Measure human flourishing outcomes (learning, capability development, creative satisfaction, time reclaimed) not just AI performance metrics.

## Risks / Limits
- AI can amplify existing expertise gaps on complex tasks — the flourishing case requires addressing access and skill-building simultaneously.
- Student overconfidence about AI literacy may create a generation that trusts AI outputs without the skills to critically evaluate them.
- Too much AI assistance can atrophy human capabilities that people value exercising — the flourishing case is for augmentation, not substitution.
- As noted in the SSIR essay on innovation, AI-driven simulation can hollow out the lived experience of creative exploration.
