# Open Questions

Unresolved questions worth revisiting as AI capabilities, adoption, and governance evolve. These are not debates to win — they are navigational questions that should be asked repeatedly, with evolving answers backed by evidence.

_5 open questions — revisited quarterly or when major evidence shifts._

---

## All Questions

### What Should Humans Keep Doing

Responsibilities, skills, relationships, and forms of judgment that should remain meaningfully human even as AI capabilities grow.

**Why It Matters:** The knowledgebase is organized around AI that expands human agency rather than treating AI as magic, inevitability, or replacement. As AI takes on more execution, the question shifts from "what can AI do?" to "what should only humans do?"

**Related:** [What Should Humans Stop Doing](#what-should-humans-stop-doing) · [What Should AI Never Decide Alone](#what-should-ai-never-decide-alone) · [[Human Agency]]

---

### What Should Humans Stop Doing

Tasks and habits humans may wisely delegate, automate, or redesign as AI makes old workflows less valuable.

**Why It Matters:** The knowledgebase is organized around AI that expands human agency rather than treating AI as magic, inevitability, or replacement. Letting go of low-value work is part of that expansion — but the distinction between delegation and surrender needs constant attention.

**Related:** [What Should Humans Keep Doing](#what-should-humans-keep-doing) · [[AI as Copilot]] · [[Future of Work]]

---

### What Should AI Never Decide Alone

Decisions that require human responsibility, consent, due process, moral judgment, or democratic legitimacy.

**Why It Matters:** The knowledgebase is organized around AI that expands human agency rather than treating AI as magic, inevitability, or replacement. Even highly capable AI systems should not make certain decisions without human oversight — the boundaries depend on context, stakes, and affected communities.

**Related:** [[Responsible Deployment]] · [[Balanced Governance]] · [[Healthcare]]

---

### What Does AI Make More Human

Examples where AI reduces drudgery or expands expression in ways that let people spend more time on relationships, judgment, creativity, and care.

**Why It Matters:** The knowledgebase is organized around AI that expands human agency rather than treating AI as magic, inevitability, or replacement. This is the counterintuitive question: which human qualities and practices does AI amplify rather than diminish?

**Related:** [[Human Agency]] · [[AI and Human Flourishing]] · [[Family and Personal Life]]

---

### What New Institutions Are Needed

Norms, public institutions, markets, educational models, and governance mechanisms that may be needed for broad AI-enabled agency.

**Why It Matters:** The knowledgebase is organized around AI that expands human agency rather than treating AI as magic, inevitability, or replacement. Some of the most important questions about AI are not technical but institutional — what new rules, organizations, and coordination mechanisms do we need?

**Related:** [[Balanced Governance]] · [[AI and Inequality]] · [[Government and Civic Life]]

## How to Use This Section

These questions are designed to be **revisited**, not answered once. As the knowledgebase grows:
- New daily digests may surface evidence that shifts the answer to a question
- Framework pages may offer structured ways to evaluate them
- Domain pages may show how answers differ by sector

If you're contributing to the wiki, consider: does your source, insight, or update change how we answer any of these five questions?
