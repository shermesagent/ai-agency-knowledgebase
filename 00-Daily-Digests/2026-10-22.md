## 3. Superagency Connections
- [[Digital Fiduciary Duty]] × [[Human Agency]]: a privacy preference must remain effective when the assistant, not the person, makes the next request.
- [[Task-Level AI Adoption]] × [[Work]]: a task-level time saving can coexist with less worker authority to set priorities or challenge a decision.
- [[Healthcare]] × [[AI Field Experiment Evidence]]: conversational access to biological hypotheses is not evidence of a validated diagnosis.

## 4. Tensions / Counterarguments
- An agent that refuses every data transfer may be safe but useless; prefer scoped, revocable permission with visible escalation rather than a blanket prohibition.
- European ad growth has multiple causes. Do not turn one earnings comparison into proof that privacy choice destroyed revenue or that agents currently place ads in recommendations.
- The job-design framework allows autonomy to rebound but provides no outcome data. Test organizational roles at multiple points rather than asserting inevitable deskilling.
- Local models can avoid some cloud transfers but still expose sensitive data through insecure devices and unsupported interpretation; no clinical use follows from a laptop demo.

## 5. Practical Applications
1. For an agent procurement review, map data category, recipient, purpose, retention, onward transfer and revocation for one **public-data-only** workflow; verify the rule persists across a connector.
2. At a workplace pilot, record who chooses, edits and signs off a task before rollout and at a scheduled later review, alongside quality and time saved. Consult affected workers.
3. Keep genomics experimentation to public, non-identifiable examples with known experimental references; require wet-lab and clinical evidence before any patient-facing claim.

## 8. Top Insight of the Day
**Delegation is a chain of authority, not a one-time click: check that privacy rules, worker discretion and evidentiary limits survive the next handoff.**

## 9. Practical Experiment to Try
On a public, non-sensitive document only, give an agent permission to summarize it but **not** share it with a third-party service. Ask it to complete a workflow that ordinarily calls an external connector. Inspect the connector request log and output: did it pause for permission, avoid the transfer or silently send data? Do not test with student, employee, patient or proprietary records. A single probe diagnoses this configuration only; it does not certify the product.
