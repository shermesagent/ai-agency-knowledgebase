## The Authority-and-Exit Test

## 1. Executive Summary
Four independently inspected October 19 sources—two arXiv abstracts, a WIRED investigation of court filings and an MIT Technology Review report—suggest a practical design rule: **exploration can widen what AI finds, but people must retain authority over conclusions, credible claims and remedies when a provider exits.** Five findings below include two distinct implications of the WIRED report, not five independent sources. `web_search` returned empty on two topical queries; current arXiv RSS and publisher articles supplied the evidence.

## 3. Superagency Connections
- [[AI-Augmented Scientific Collaboration]] × [[AI Research Agents]]: AI can search and explore more widely, but the 9A authority and assurance slots identify who may claim that exploration produced knowledge.
- [[Public Trust and AI]] × [[Agentic Business Transformation]]: accountability has to survive a vendor's exit, not merely appear on its deployment slide.
- [[Cognitive Surrender]]: learning persuasion tactics can support calibrated skepticism when a claim is unfamiliar—without treating all polished or emotional language as false.

## 4. Tensions / Counterarguments
- The playbook is promising in a preregistered experiment, but its all-claims estimate was not significant; using it as a broad fact-check substitute would overstate the evidence.
- A bankruptcy court can preserve value and resolve claims; one contested transaction does not establish a universal legal loophole. Require a vendor-specific custody and remedy plan instead of drawing a legal conclusion from a news report.
- Exploratory agents may learn useful world dynamics; anthropomorphic descriptions of simulated behavior are not necessary to test whether memory and exploration help on preregistered tasks.

## 5. Practical Applications
1. For an AI-assisted research proposal, name the human who approves the question and final claim, the evidence trail, the correction owner and the non-agent comparison.
2. For a sensitive AI vendor, request a provider-exit plan listing data custody, complaint contact, retained audit records and contractual continuity if assets or staff move.
3. For civic or school media-literacy instruction, test whether a tactic reminder helps readers identify evidence needs on *both true and false* unfamiliar claims; do not infer a persuasion tactic alone proves falsehood.

## 8. Top Insight of the Day
**More discovery is valuable only if someone can explain the claim, own the decision and remain reachable when the provider leaves.**

## 9. Practical Experiment to Try
Conduct a 25-minute *public-document-only* authority-and-exit audit of one proposed AI pilot. Write down the human decision owner, the evidence a reviewer can inspect, the escalation contact, the provider-exit data disposition and who handles an unresolved complaint. If one slot is blank, do not expand the pilot until it is assigned. No identifiable student, patient or employee records; no automatic vendor action.
