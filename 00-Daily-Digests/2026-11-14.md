# Daily AI Agency Digest — 2026-11-14

## Executive Summary
Five readings connect an unusual mix of creator work, agent security, autonomy thresholds and aviation. The recurring question is not whether AI can produce an impressive output; it is **who retains a real stop, restore, permission and compensation path afterward**. This is a weekend reading/curation pass, not five independent new studies. Sources are journalism, practical advice and commentary; company claims are attributed rather than treated as proven outcomes.

## Top 5 Findings / Best Sources Found

1. **A proof of concept can increase representation while reducing paid roles.** [WIRED on Mansa's AI microdrama](https://www.wired.com/story/black-roles-are-disappearing-in-hollywood-this-startup-is-using-ai-microdramas-to-fight-back/) reports a Black-led AI-generated production meant to demonstrate demand; creators dispute the use of no human actors. Future staffed commissions are the company's aspiration, not verified results. → [[AI and Creator Rights]], [[AI and Inequality]].
2. **Review the permission graph before granting an agent a connector.** [WIRED's shared-link audit guide](https://www.wired.com/story/audit-all-the-cloud-storage-links-youve-shared/) details a practical way to revisit persistent cloud-sharing links. It is a how-to, not a study of AI-agent leakage; the extension to agent permissions is our operational inference. → [[Digital Fiduciary Duty]], [[Responsible Deployment]].
3. **AI-enabled crime still requires basic recovery discipline.** [WIRED on AI-fueled ransomware](https://www.wired.com/story/beware-the-new-ai-fueled-ransomware-boom/) frames the evolving threat. The title is not evidence of local prevalence or AI as a necessary cause. Inventory credentials and backups, test restore and session revocation before adding agents to sensitive workflows. → [[Digital Fiduciary Duty]], [[Sandbox Integrity]].
4. **A system card needs an action threshold.** [Zvi Mowshowitz's assessment of the Fable/Mythos 5.2 card](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/fable-and-mythos-52-the-system-card) challenges an autonomy threshold while accepting some of the company's other judgments. These are contested interpretations, not independent safety validation. Ask which evaluation result would change deployment. → [[Balanced Governance]], [[Human Review Checkpoints]].
5. **A supervised demonstration is not unsupervised safety proof.** [WIRED's aviation reporting](https://www.wired.com/story/no-pilots-few-air-traffic-controllers-what-could-go-wrong/) describes a test flight with a safety pilot aboard and the limits of replacing pilots/controllers. It explicitly says there is no evidence AI caused the November 13 national groundstop. Avoid treating either event as evidence for the other; specify the fallback and its owner. → [[Balanced Governance]], [[Responsible Deployment]].

## Superagency Connections
[[Human Agency]] requires more than access to generation. A creator needs a route to compensation; an agent user needs a way to revoke permissions; a deployer needs a threshold and fallback. [[Digital Fiduciary Duty]] and [[Balanced Governance]] express those requirements at different scales.

## Tensions / Counterarguments
A successful AI prototype may open a market otherwise closed to a creator, but neither market visibility nor audience response proves creators or performers benefit. A flight with a human safety pilot can build evidence without proving an unattended system is ready. Security advice is precautionary; the reporting here does not quantify how much AI changes the ransomware baseline.

## Practical Applications
- Before an internal assistant pilot, audit existing shared links, connector scopes and revocation procedures with the data owner; avoid student records in any ad hoc test.
- Put a named stop criterion and accountable owner beside each proposed AI demonstration or evaluation.
- For creative pilots, define who gets paid if a prototype is commissioned and measure actual follow-on employment.

## Top Insight of the Day
**An AI pilot's real deliverable is not just the output; it is the auditable path to withdraw access, correct mistakes and distribute the upside.**

## Practical Experiment to Try
On a sandbox folder containing only non-sensitive, owner-approved documents, grant a test agent read-only access. Record who approved it, which links already existed, when access expires, and how quickly the owner can remove access and confirm revocation. Do not connect actual student, staff or confidential district data. Repeat the test with a simulated error: can a human stop the agent before it shares anything? This tests the governance process, not model accuracy.
