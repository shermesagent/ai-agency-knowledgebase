# Books

Curated source index for books relevant to AI agency, Superagency, practical adoption, and responsible optimism.

## Accepted Sources
- **[Superagency: What Could Possibly Go Right with Our AI Future](https://www.superagency.ai/)** — Reid Hoffman with Greg Beato, 2025. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. Foundational optimistic thesis for AI as an agency-expanding technology. Related: [[Superagency]], [[Human Agency]], [[Optimism Without Naivety]].
- **[Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/741805/co-intelligence-by-ethan-mollick/)** — Ethan Mollick, 2024. Reliability 5/5; relevance 5/5. Practical guide to human-AI collaboration and learning-by-using AI in real work. Related: [[AI as Copilot]], [[Practical AI]], [[Work]].
- **[Brave New Words](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/740806/brave-new-words-by-salman-khan/)** — Salman Khan, 2024. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. Practitioner case for AI tutoring as frequent feedback and teacher-supported personalization. Related: [[Education]], [[AI Tutors]], [[AI for School Districts]].
- **[AI as Normal Technology / AI Snake Oil](https://www.normaltech.ai/)** — Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor, 2024/ongoing. Reliability 5/5; relevance 5/5. Skeptical, evidence-focused counterweight to hype and doom. Related: [[AI as Normal Technology]], [[Strongest AI Risk Arguments]], [[Responsible Deployment]].
