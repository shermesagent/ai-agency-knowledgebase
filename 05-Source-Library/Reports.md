# Reports

Curated source index for reports, standards, and policy frameworks relevant to AI agency, Superagency, practical adoption, and responsible optimism.

## Accepted Sources
- **[AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** — NIST, 2023/2024; page updated 2026. Reliability 5/5; relevance 5/5. Map, Measure, Manage, Govern framework for responsible AI deployment. Related: [[Responsible Deployment]], [[Responsible Deployment Loop]], [[Risk-Benefit Matrix]].
- **[Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning](https://www.ed.gov/sites/ed/files/documents/ai-report/ai-report.pdf)** — U.S. Department of Education, 2023. Reliability 5/5; relevance 5/5. Education guidance covering teaching, learning, assessment, equity, privacy, and human judgment. Related: [[Education]], [[AI Tutors]], [[AI for School Districts]].
- **[Computational Power and AI](https://ainowinstitute.org/publications/compute-and-ai)** — Jai Vipra and Sarah Myers West, AI Now Institute, 2023-09-27. Reliability 4/5; relevance 4/5. Policy analysis of compute infrastructure, supply chains, and concentration risks. Related: [[AI and Inequality]], [[Balanced Governance]], [[Strongest AI Risk Arguments]].
- **[Guidance for Safe Foundation Model Deployment](https://partnershiponai.org/wp-content/uploads/1923/10/PAI-Model-Deployment-Guidance.pdf)** — Partnership on AI, 2023. Reliability 4/5; relevance 4/5. Multi-stakeholder deployment guidance for foundation model providers. Related: [[Responsible Deployment]], [[Balanced Governance]], [[Risk-Benefit Matrix]].
- **[The Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index)** — Anthropic, 2025/2026. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. Research/data tool about AI usage across occupations and collaboration versus delegation. Related: [[Work]], [[Future of Work]], [[Agency Expansion Framework]].
