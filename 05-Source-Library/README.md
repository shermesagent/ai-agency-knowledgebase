# Source Library

Indexed sources by type — the evidence backbone of the knowledgebase. Each page catalogs accepted sources with URLs, reliability scores, relevance scores, and short summaries. Sources are added by the daily research curator and stored in `/sources/sources.jsonl`.

**9 index pages** — sources are added daily and deduplicated.

---

## All Pages

- [[Articles]] — news articles, blog posts, essays, and analysis pieces
- [[Papers]] — academic papers, preprints, and research reports
- [[Books]] — published books and book-length treatments
- [[Interviews and Podcasts]] — podcast episodes, interviews with researchers, practitioners, and domain experts
- [[Reports]] — institutional reports, white papers, and policy documents
- [[Repositories and Tools]] — AI tools, platforms, software, code repositories, datasets, and open-source projects referenced in wiki pages
- [[Knowledgebase Tool Comparison]] — comparison of wiki/knowledgebase hosting platforms (Git-backed, Obsidian, Wiki.js, DokuWiki)

---

## Source Standards

All sources must meet the quality bar defined in [[SCHEMA]]:
- **Reliability score** 1–5
- **Relevance score** 1–5
- Preferred: both scores ≥ 4
- Reject: SEO posts, generic hype, duplicate coverage, pure marketing, alarmism without insight
