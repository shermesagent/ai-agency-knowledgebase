- **[The Consistency Dilemma in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30653)** — arXiv, 2026-07-01. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. Counterintuitive finding: more self-consistent models are MORE vulnerable to mistakes. Consistency is achieved by suppressing internal uncertainty signals — the very signals that would alert human reviewers. A model giving the same wrong answer 10 times is more dangerous than one giving varied answers.
