- **[The Consistency Dilemma in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30653)** — arXiv, 2026-07-01. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. - **[The Veto Variable: Human Override as a Goal-Independent Cost Term](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00109)** — arXiv, 2026-09-02. Reliability 4/5; relevance 5/5. The reassurance that benign terminal goals will behave accordingly fails structurally: for a settled-goal capable agent, continued human oversight is an uncontrolled variable (a standing possibility the goal is revoked), imposing a goal-independent discount on goals that don't constitutively require human welfare. Welfare-preservation ≠ veto-preservation; an additively aggregative welfare goal charges only a |H_v|/|H_w|-scaled debit for capturing the veto-holders.
