# A Flaw in ChatGPT’s Mac App Could Have Let Hackers Grab Sensitive Data

Publisher: WIRED. Published: 2026-10-02. Authors: Lily Hay Newman.

Source note (paraphrase of directly read publisher article or arXiv abstract; not full text): WIRED reports Objective-See found a patched local-process vulnerability in the ChatGPT macOS application. A trusted interpreter could relay a malicious local script past process-signature checks, potentially exposing chat logs or enabling app-mediated actions. OpenAI acknowledged a fix on September 25; the article does not establish actual compromise of users. The deployment lesson is to constrain local privileges as well as model permissions.
