# AI Is Making a Mess of Nurses’ Schedules. They Say It’s a Safety Issue

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/ai-making-mess-of-nurses-schedules-they-say-its-a-safety-issue/
Published: 2026-10-02
Author(s): Paresh Dave
Capture: curator source note from publisher page read 2026-10-03; original article not reproduced.

WIRED interviews nurses who say HCA’s Palantir-built scheduling tool produces unwanted shift patterns and weak skill mix, while HCA says nursing leaders retain final decisions and cites efficiency and staffing metrics. Reported scheduling failures and care concerns have not been established as causal patient outcomes. A local override reportedly became a centralized appeal; evaluate preference exceptions, actual staffing mix, fatigue and appeal time, not just manager hours saved.
