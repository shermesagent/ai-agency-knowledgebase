# Don’t be fooled—LLMs don’t reason

Publisher: MIT Technology Review. Published: 2026-10-02. Authors: Thore Graepel.

Source note (paraphrase of directly read publisher article or arXiv abstract; not full text): Graepel, a former AlphaGo team member, contrasts explicit game-tree search and revised evidence states with current language-model chain-of-thought. He argues trustworthy high-stakes systems need an inspectable epistemic state, not just fluent intermediate text. This is a substantive architectural argument using a contested definition of reasoning, not a controlled comparison demonstrating every LLM system lacks useful inference.
