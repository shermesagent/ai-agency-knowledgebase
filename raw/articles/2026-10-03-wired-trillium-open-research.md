# These AI Experts Want to Do High-Stakes Research Out in the Open

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/trillium-labs-wants-to-do-high-risk-ai-research-in-the-open/
Published: 2026-10-02
Author(s): Will Knight
Capture: curator source note from publisher page read 2026-10-03; original article not reproduced.

WIRED reports Nathan Lambert and Tom Zick launched Trillium Labs to publish replicable experiments on post-training, reinforcement learning and potentially recursive self-improvement. Founders argue openness enables outside scrutiny; access-control proponents see dual-use risk. Funding aspirations and research plans are not completed experiments or evidence of safer outcomes; the governing question is what to disclose, when, and under what independent review.
