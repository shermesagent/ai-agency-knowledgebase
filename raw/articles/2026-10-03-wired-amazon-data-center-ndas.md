# Amazon Says It Is Going to Stop Using NDAs for Data Centers

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/amazon-says-it-is-going-to-stop-using-ndas-for-data-centers/
Published: 2026-10-02
Author(s): Molly Taft
Capture: curator source note from publisher page read 2026-10-03; original article not reproduced.

Amazon says it has stopped using nondisclosure agreements with county officials on data-center deals and announces a five-year community investment pledge. WIRED documents prior opacity, emerging government restrictions on such agreements, and the unresolved question of whether the new policy reaches contractors. This is an announced commitment, not proof every project has disclosed water, energy, subsidy, and end-tenant terms.
