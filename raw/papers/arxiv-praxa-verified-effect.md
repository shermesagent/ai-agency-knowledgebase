# From Proposal to Verified Effect: Praxa, an Evidence-Bound Harness for Governed AI Agent Execution

Publisher: arXiv. Published: 2026-07-27. Authors: Stefan G. Creadore.

Source note (paraphrase of directly read publisher article or arXiv abstract; not full text): An author-run engineering preprint distinguishes agent proposal, approval, dispatch, externally read-back effect, and reviewed promotion. Its small Terminal-Bench pilot had identical 17/36 strict pass counts in both arms while the reliability layer consumed more tokens. The architecture clarifies which claims require independent checks, but its author-run results do not establish general superiority, security, or user benefit; its October feed appearance is not its July submission date.
