# When the AI Leaves the Tailorshop: Measuring What an LLM Advisor Leaves Behind in Complex Problem Solving

Publisher: arXiv. Published: 2026-09-14. Authors: Robin Welsch.

Source note (paraphrase of directly read publisher article or arXiv abstract; not full text): Two preregistered simulated-factory experiments (N=200 and N=198) examine assisted decisions, confidence, knowledge, and performance after the LLM advisor is withdrawn. Assisted users reported higher confidence at less effort, but objective outcomes varied by study and comparator. More frequent recommendation changes correlated with better unaided outcomes within assisted groups, not proof of a causal learning mechanism; this is not K–12 field evidence.
